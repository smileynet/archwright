# Projections Are One-Way

## Rule

Every generated surface (web HTML, markdown) carries the generator banner
`generated by archwright-report — do not edit`, and surfaces are only ever
produced by regenerating from the canonical JSON bundle. No hand edits, no
sideways merges.

## Rationale

`canonical-doc-projections`: humans and agents must never see contradictory
truths. The moment a surface is edited directly, it carries information absent
from the canonical document, and the projection guarantee (invariant 1 of the
model) is dead. The banner makes generated status self-declaring and gives this
check its mechanical hook.

## Violations Look Like

```html
<!-- BAD — a surface file with no generator banner (hand-created or stripped): -->
<html><body><h1>Snackbox — Design Check</h1> ... hand-tuned copy ... </body></html>
```

## Correct Usage

```html
<!-- GOOD — first line of every generated surface: -->
<!-- generated by archwright-report — do not edit; regenerate from the canonical JSON bundle -->
```
