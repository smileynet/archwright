# Refund On Cancel

## Statement

A customer can cancel at any point before dispensing begins and get their full inserted balance back.

## Who Feels It

The customer who changed their mind — or inserted coins for an item that turns out to be sold out. Walking away from trapped money is the "machine ate my coins" story in its most common form.

## Evidence

- L2: usability sessions show cancel-before-commit is attempted in roughly 1 in 12 interactions (wrong selection, price surprise).
- Soft: HOW promptly the refund returns is negotiable; THAT it returns in full is not — the full-refund half overlaps `fair-exchange` (a sibling desire, resolved together by `pattern:payment-gate`).
