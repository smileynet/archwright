# Fair Exchange

## Statement

A customer always leaves the kiosk with exactly one of: the snack they paid for, or all of their money back — never neither, never both.

## Who Feels It

The customer, at the machine, with coins already inserted. Every other force in this design exists to serve this one.

## Evidence

- Product-level desire (L4): the kiosk's entire reputation is "it never eats your money". A single swallowed coin loses a customer permanently.
- Trading-standards rules for vending treat kept-payment-without-goods as the operator's liability — the desire has regulatory teeth.
